  subject of Module 13."  It is evaluated here.

    omega chi/c^2               = 7.130652e-01

    THE CRITERION IS << 1, NOT < 1, AND 0.7131 IS NEITHER.
    At order unity the compression leaks about one radian's worth
    of heat per radian of oscillation, so the mode is neither
    adiabatic nor isothermal.  module04.html:563 predicted exactly
    this in print -- "fails near the photosphere where radiation
    carries heat out of a compression within a period" -- and
    0.7131 is that failure AT THE COLUMN-MEAN OPACITY OF PART C,
    used as if it were local; the ATLAS9 values follow.
    *** chi above carries the COLUMN-MEAN opacity of PART C, and

    *** Module 6's and Module 4's values):
    ***   T = Teff     chi = 1.5027e+13  omega chi/c^2 = 0.4468
    ***   tau_R = 2/3  chi = 1.1587e+13  omega chi/c^2 = 0.3445
    *** Both lie between 0.3445 and 0.4468: below 1, and not << 1.
    *** CHECK 3 VERDICT (Gate D addendum): the adiabatic closure is
    *** REFUTED at the photosphere, and module04.html:429's own
    *** prediction that it fails there is CONFIRMED.  The module
    *** prints BOTH levels; 0.7131 above is the column-mean value and
    *** is superseded, printed only to show what CHECK 2 moved. ***

    The same at the acoustic cutoff, nu_ac = 4497 microHz:
      omega chi/c^2 = 1.037752e+00  (ratio to nu_max: 1.4553)

    THE SAME NUMBER, READ AS TWO TIMES.  omega chi/c^2 is not a
    scale-height statement at all: with a wavelength lam = c/nu,
    the diffusion time across one wavelength divided by one period
    is c^2/(nu chi), and omega chi/c^2 is 2 pi divided by it.  The
    two must therefore satisfy one identity, which is the check:
      wavelength at nu_max   = 2.6152e+08 cm = 2615.2 km
      diffusion time across it = 2.8516e+03 s
      the period               = 3.2362e+02 s
      ratio (diffusion/period) = 8.8115e+00
      2 pi/ratio               = 0.713065
      omega chi/c^2            = 0.713065
      IDENTICAL, so the two readings are one quantity.  Adiabatic
      wants the ratio LARGE; 8.81 periods is not large.
