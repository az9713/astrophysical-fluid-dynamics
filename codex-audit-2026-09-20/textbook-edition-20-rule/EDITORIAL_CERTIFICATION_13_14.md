## What changed, and why

| Module | Revision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | Rebuilt the opening and main expository paragraphs around radiation moments, transfer, diffusion, the atmosphere, and radiative force. | The original repeatedly narrated the book's production history instead of teaching the physical argument. |
| 13 | Recast the photospheric $\omega\chi/c_s^2$ calculation as a local leakage estimate. | Diffusion at Rosseland optical depth $2/3$ does not justify a definitive refutation of an adiabatic oscillation model. A damping rate requires non-adiabatic transfer and a boundary condition. |
| 13 | Kept the inferred $0.15231\,\mathrm{cm^2\,g^{-1}}$ as a **column mean**, and reported the ATLAS9 ratios without a validation verdict. | A tolerance chosen after seeing the result is descriptive, not a blind acceptance test. A column mean is not the local opacity. |
| 13 | Restricted electron-scattering statements and the $m_p$ Eddington formula to fully ionised gas in the low-energy Thomson regime. | At higher photon energies, the Klein–Nishina cross-section depends on energy; the flux-mean opacity is then spectrum-dependent. The hydrogen fraction is a parameter, not a universal black-hole accretion composition. |
| 13 | Replaced the Sagittarius A* “efficiency” inference with a band-limited luminosity to outer-supply comparison. | A 2–10 keV luminosity divided by a Bondi supply power does not measure bolometric efficiency at the black hole. The worked problem now respects that limit. |
| 14 | Rebuilt the exposition around conservation, stability, modified equations, Riemann solutions, convergence, and resolution. | The original prose mixed numerical findings with draft history and categorical verdicts. |
| 14 | Reported SPH pressure error in both maximum and volume-weighted relative $L_1$ measures. | The maximum error does not fall over three resolutions, while the $L_1$ error does. A norm-free statement of non-convergence was false. |
| 14 | Described $\mathrm{Re}\lesssim N^{4/3}$ as a one-scale grid estimate and revised Problem C3. | It is not a theorem about every numerical representation or an accuracy guarantee. |
| 14 | Reported the finite-grid Sedov shock radii and formal-order comparison as convergence results. | Conservation does not make shock position exact at finite resolution. A discontinuity does not invalidate a scheme's formal order under its smooth-solution assumptions. |
